In three lines

On the birthday itself, Saturday 3 October: a boat at Lee Valley if the group will work locks, otherwise the Wyndham charter at Ferry Meadows.

Birthday weekend, Sunday 4 October: Union Wharf at Market Harborough. The best October boat: no locks, heaters, dog welcome.

Celebrate a week early, Saturday 26 September: Fox Narrowboats at March. The best boat of the lot, closest, no locks.

The birthday weekend, 3 and 4 October

Saturday is the day itself. Fox has closed for the year by now, so the boats are an hour away.

Lee Valley Boat Centre, Broxbourne Boat free · £300

The only boat free on the birthday itself. Seats 12, toilet, dog free. The catch: there are no lock-keepers, so you work two or three locks each way, and the Lee is busier and more built-up than the Fens. If nobody fancies locks, do the Wyndham charter instead.

The best October day. Moorhen seats 12 with a heater and roll-down sides; Water Vole seats exactly six with radiators and a table for £200. Fixed lock-free route to Foxton and back, dog welcome, 4.9 stars from 287 reviews. Ask which boat boards more easily, one reviewer found the steps steep.

How to book, and what to ask

Fox Narrowboats

01354 652770 · 10 Marina Drive, March PE15 0AU

  • Is the 26th or 27th still free on either boat, and can we hold it now?
  • Is there a heater? Your site lists hob, fridge and toilet but not heating.
  • How does an elderly passenger board? Steps, handrail, help?
  • Any weed or low-water warning on the Benwick route that week?

£100 deposit, £10 for the dog, two adults must sign, fuel and tuition included. Refund if cancelled 28 days ahead.

Union Wharf

01858 432123 · Market Harborough LE16 7UW

  • Moorhen or Water Vole on Sunday 4 October: which boards more easily?
  • Is there a backrest at the tiller? One reviewer found none on Moorhen.
  • Which pub at the foot of Foxton takes dogs?

Return 4.30, or 4 in darker months. Prices only show once you pick a date in their booking widget.

The day at Fox, if it is the 26th

08.30Leave Coton. Free parking at the yard. 09.30Handover and tuition. Arrive before 10. 10.15West along the Old River Nene toward Benwick. No locks. 12.15Moor at Benwick. Five Alls pub at weekends, or the cafe in the Spar, or a picnic. 14.00Turn and amble back. Kettle on board. 16.30Back at the yard, well inside the 6pm return.

Nan and the dog

  • Covered front seating and a dinette, so Nan sits in the warm all day. Bring cushions and a blanket; nobody has confirmed a heater.
  • Flush toilet on board.
  • Dog on a lead on board and at the pub.
  • Bring photo ID, layers, a backup picnic, binoculars.

Everything else, in one line each

  • Purvis Marine, Huntingdon (01480 453628), 21 minutes. Closes end of September and publishes nothing about boat size, dogs or price. Ring if you want a boat close to home on the 26th or 27th.
  • Fish and Duck, near Ely and Brandon Creek, Littleport: no dogs allowed. Out.
  • Cosgrove Narrowboats: open every date at £210, lock-free, but 79 minutes each way and no dog policy published.
  • MK Afloat, Milton Keynes: struck on safety reviews. Ouse Boats, Bedford and Peterborough Boat Hire: websites dead.
  • Nene Valley Railway (45 min): steam with an accessible coach, dog £2, £122 for six on a standard day. A sound third non-boat option for any weekend.
  • Shuttleworth Race Day Air Show, Sunday 4 October, from £35 a head: a real occasion, but dogs on air-show days unconfirmed, 01767 627927.
  • Rejected non-boat ideas: Burghley, Anglesey Abbey, Welney, Bressingham, Stotfold Mill and both vineyards ban or exclude the dog; Wicken Fen, Wimpole and anything in Cambridge or Ely are too familiar.
  • Rivers this autumn: East Anglia is in drought, so expect low water and weed rather than fast currents. No lock closures on any date above. Check 0345 988 1188 the week before.
